Add `@allons-y/opx-lint-ts`, the lint detector for TypeScript (`.ts`, `.tsx`, `.mts`, `.cts`). It bundles ESLint, `typescript-eslint`, and `@stylistic/eslint-plugin`, and requires `typescript` as a peer dependency. Enable it with `"ts": true` under `lint` in `.opx/config.json`.
